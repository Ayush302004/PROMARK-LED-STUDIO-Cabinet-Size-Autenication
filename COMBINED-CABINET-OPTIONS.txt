PROMARK LED STUDIO

The application name is now PROMARK LED STUDIO.

PM1.2 / 1.25 SMD and COB are each listed once, with these cabinet sizes:
- 600 × 337.5 × 38.1 mm
- 640 × 480 × 70 mm

PM1.5 SMD and COB are each listed once, with these cabinet sizes:
- 600 × 337.5 × 39.75 mm
- 640 × 480 × 70 mm

PM1.8 duplicate listings are also combined into one model, with the existing cabinet options retained. Other models and all controllers remain available. The catalogue has 8 unique model listings.

Choose Cabinet size in Screen Settings or in the catalogue card. Changing cabinet size preserves your requested screen dimensions and recalculates the cabinet layout and quotation. Saved designs using the previous model IDs are mapped to the correct size option.

Existing price rates are retained. The new 640 mm PM1.2 / 1.25 SMD option uses the existing 1.2 / 1.25 rate; its unprovided native pixel resolution remains Not provided.

Extract the ZIP, restart the app, and press Ctrl+F5.
