Quotation items:
1. Video wall — existing automatic screen-area price
2. Controller — supplied base rate × 130 × 2 × quantity, before GST
3. Mounting Structure — blank manual Rate cell F12
4. Installation — blank manual Rate cell F13
5. UPS — blank manual Rate cell F14
6. Cabling — blank manual Rate cell F15

Items 3–6 use the description As per the company actual price. Their rates are not derived from screen area, and old structure/installation rates in saved designs are ignored.

All Unit cells default to Nos. Enter company actual prices directly into the yellow Rate cells in Excel. Taxes and totals update once prices are entered. Enter 0 for an included/no-charge item. Blank prices keep the final total pending.
