Video Resolution shows the whole-wall pixel width and height. Total pixels = pixel width x pixel height, also shown in millions. Automatic controller selection is on for new designs and chooses the smallest supplied LED Wall Controller capacity strictly above the wall total, within its supplied maximum pixel width and height. For example, 3.1 million pixels fits a 3.9 million pixel controller if width and height also fit. Selecting a controller manually turns automatic selection off; check the automatic option to enable it again. Existing saved designs retain their manual controller unless saved with automatic selection enabled. Models without native pixel counts cannot be matched. Multimedia players are available manually but are not automatically substituted for video controllers. Confirm inputs, receiving cards and output-port wiring for the installation. Selected controller descriptions and existing pricing carry through to Excel; no prices are invented.
