PRO MARK AV STUDIO — EXCEL QUOTATION + COB / SMD

1. Extract this ZIP into a new folder.
2. Run START-HERE.bat, or install requirements.txt and run python app.py.
3. Use All / SMD / COB under Display & installation.
   All retains original models; SMD and COB show supplied PM variants.
4. Design the wall, then open View results > Excel quotation.
5. Enter client/project details, your rates, GST and quantities.
6. Click Download Excel quotation. The .xlsx is editable in Excel.

The video wall row is 1 complete system (Nos). Enter the complete wall rate,
not a cabinet unit price. The calculated cabinet quantity and buildable wall
size are written into its description. Other rows use their own quantities.
Yellow cells in Excel are editable. Empty rates keep the total pending;
enter 0 deliberately for included/no-charge items. Excel recalculates formulas.
GST defaults to 18% from the reference and can be changed.
Company CIN, GSTIN and PAN are editable and are not inferred from the image.
The sample screenshot's prices and controller capacity are not reused.
Controller selection is manual until validated controller specs are provided.

The supplied PM technical tables drive the new variant calculations. PM1.2
uses 600 x 337.5 mm / 480 x 270 pixels. PM1.5 has separate COB and SMD ratings.
PM3.9 uses the sheet's printed 4 mm pitch, giving a derived 240 x 240 cabinet
resolution; confirm this against the model label before ordering.
Original catalogue models and slider behavior are also available under All.

The example Excel file in examples is unpriced and illustrates the format.
